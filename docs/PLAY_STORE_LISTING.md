# Piggy Play Store listing

## App name

Piggy: Savings & Price Watch

## Short description

Plan a purchase, track your savings, and get alerts when its price drops.

## Full description

Plan what you want. Save at your pace. Buy when the price is right.

Piggy brings your savings plan and the price of the thing you want into one clear place. Create a goal, choose a target and timeline, record what you save, and see the next step that keeps you moving.

When you are saving for a specific product, attach its secure product-page link. Piggy checks the public price and can alert you when it reaches the amount you chose, so you can decide when it is time to buy.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Create purchase and savings goals
• Record contributions and see your progress fill up
• Build a daily, weekly, monthly, or flexible saving plan
• Get saving reminders and pace forecasts
• Track a product price alongside its goal
• Receive push alerts when the observed price reaches your target
• Add goal widgets to your Android home screen
• Back up and sync your records with a Piggy account
• Export clear PDF and CSV reports
• Personalize Piggy with different themes and goal colors

YOUR MONEY STAYS YOURS

Piggy is a planning and record-keeping tool. It is not a bank, wallet, investment platform, broker, lender, or financial adviser. Piggy never receives, holds, transfers, or invests your money. The amounts shown in Piggy are records you enter; your actual funds remain wherever you choose to keep them.

Price checks use publicly available merchant pages. Prices and availability can change, and some merchants may block automated checks. Always confirm the final price and seller terms before buying.

## Positioning guardrails

- Describe recorded amounts as progress or records, never as money held by Piggy.
- Do not call Piggy a bank, wallet, investment product, or payment service.
- Do not promise merchant coverage, guaranteed price accuracy, returns, or guaranteed savings outcomes.
- Show the non-custodial explanation anywhere users may reasonably think money enters the app.
