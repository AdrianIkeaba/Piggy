PIGGY / LEGAL

Privacy policy

Effective 9 September 2026

This policy explains how Piggy handles information when you use the Piggy Android application and its related cloud services. Piggy is a savings planning and record-keeping tool. It does not hold, transfer, or invest your money.

Information stored on your device

Piggy stores the information you enter so the app works offline. This can include goal names, target amounts and dates, chosen colors, saving plans, deposit or withdrawal records, notes, app appearance, currency preference, widget choices, and reminder settings.

Information collected when you use cloud features

You can use core saving features without an account. If you create or sign in to an account, use price alerts, or purchase Piggy Plus, Piggy may collect:

  • Your email address and a unique account identifier.
  • Your synced goals, targets, balances, transaction records, notes, and saving plans.
  • Product names, secure product-page URLs, target prices, check frequency, observed prices, and price-check timestamps for price watches.
  • A Firebase Cloud Messaging device token used to deliver price alerts.
  • Subscription product, entitlement status, store, and expiry information. Piggy does not receive your full card or bank details.
  • Basic technical request information processed by hosting and security providers, such as timestamps and network information.

How information is used

  • Provide authentication, cloud backup, synchronization, reminders, widgets, exports, price tracking, and subscription access.
  • Send transactional messages such as sign-in codes and requested price-drop alerts.
  • Protect accounts, enforce plan limits, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and maintain service reliability.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

Service providers

Piggy uses service providers to operate the app: Supabase for authentication, database storage, and server functions; Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications; RevenueCat for subscription entitlement management; Google Play for app distribution and billing; and Google authentication if you choose Google sign-in. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments. Piggy does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Data security

Data sent between Piggy and its cloud services is encrypted in transit. Cloud records use account-based access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but Piggy uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service.

Retention and deletion

Guest data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Cloud account data is retained while your account is active. Price history remains until its price watch or account is deleted. Subscription transaction records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own legal and operational requirements.

You can permanently delete your Piggy account and associated Piggy cloud data from Profile - Cloud backup - Delete account and data. You can also use the external account deletion page. Local data is removed by the app when deletion succeeds; clearing app storage removes any remaining device copy.

Your choices

You may use Piggy without signing in, decline notification permission, pause or delete price watches, export your savings records, sign out, or delete your account. Android settings let you control notification access and clear local app data.

Children

Piggy is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so it can be removed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Piggy changes. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email piggy.auth@gmail.com.
