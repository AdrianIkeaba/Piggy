PIGGY / DATA CONTROL

Delete selected data

Keep your account, remove the records you choose

You can remove selected Piggy data without deleting your account. Deleting an item removes its cloud-synced copy when you are signed in and removes its local copy from your device.

Delete in the app

  • Open a savings goal, transaction, saving plan, or price watch.
  • Choose the edit or management action.
  • Select the available delete option and confirm.

You can also pause or delete price watches, remove saving plans, and clear individual savings records while keeping your Piggy account.

Request help with selected data

If you cannot access the app, email us from the Piggy account email address. Use the subject Piggy selected data deletion request, explain which data you want removed, and do not include passwords, one-time codes, card details, or API keys. We may ask for a limited verification step before processing the request.

REQUEST SELECTED DATA DELETION

What may remain

Google Play and RevenueCat may retain purchase and subscription records for billing, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, or legal compliance. Those records are governed by their own policies. To remove all Piggy cloud data and your account, use the account deletion page.

Timing

In-app deletion is processed immediately when the service is available. Verified emailed requests are normally completed within 30 days.
