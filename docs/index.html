PIGGY / PLAN WITH PURPOSE

Plan what you want. Save at your pace. Buy when the price is right.

Piggy turns a purchase into a clear savings plan, keeps your progress visible, and watches the product price for you. Your money always stays wherever you choose to keep it.

01 · PLAN

Create a goal with a target and timeline you can actually follow.

02 · RECORD

Log what you save while the real money stays in your bank, wallet, or account.

03 · WATCH

Link the product page and let Piggy alert you when its public price reaches your target.

Piggy is a planning and record-keeping tool. It is not a bank, wallet, investment platform, broker, or lender. Piggy never receives, holds, transfers, or invests your money.
