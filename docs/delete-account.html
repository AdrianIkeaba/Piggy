PIGGY / DATA CONTROL

Delete your account

Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your Piggy account and the Piggy cloud data associated with it.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Piggy and select Profile.
  2. Select Cloud backup.
  3. Select Delete account and data.
  4. Review the warning and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email the address below from the email account you use with Piggy. Use the subject Piggy account deletion request. We may reply with a limited verification step to prevent unauthorized deletion.

REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION

What is deleted

  • Your Piggy authentication account and profile.
  • Cloud-synced goals, saving plans, transaction records, and notes.
  • Price watches, price history, and registered push-notification tokens.
  • Piggy subscription entitlement records associated with the account.

What may remain elsewhere

Google Play and RevenueCat may retain purchase and subscription records as required for billing, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, or legal compliance. Those records are governed by their respective policies. Data stored only on a device can be removed by clearing Piggy's app storage or uninstalling Piggy.

Timing

In-app deletion is processed immediately when the service is available. Emailed requests are handled after account ownership is verified. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.
