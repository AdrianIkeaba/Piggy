PIGGY / HOW IT WORKS

Plan it. Save for it. Catch the right price.

Piggy for Android

Contact support
Email piggy.auth@gmail.com with a short description of the issue, your Android version, and the Piggy version shown by Google Play. Do not send passwords, email sign-in codes, card details, or API keys.

Why use Piggy?

Piggy connects the thing you want to buy with a realistic plan to afford it. You can create a target, record progress, see whether your saving pace is on track, receive reminders, and watch the public product price from the same place. Piggy helps you make a patient purchase decision; it does not offer an account to fund or an investment product to buy.

Is Piggy a bank or investment platform?

No. Piggy is a savings planner and purchase tracker. It is not a bank, wallet, financial institution, investment platform, broker, lender, or financial adviser. It does not accept deposits, connect to your bank account, move funds, promise returns, or invest money.

Where is my money?

Piggy records saving progress; it does not receive, hold, or transfer money. Your actual funds remain wherever you choose to keep them.

Why record savings if Piggy does not hold them?

A visible record turns a vague purchase wish into a measurable plan. Piggy calculates progress from the amounts you enter, helps you keep a routine, and shows the next useful step. Your Piggy record should be treated as a planning aid and checked against wherever you actually keep the money.

How does price tracking work?

Attach one secure product-page link to a savings goal and set the price you want. Piggy periodically reads the public price shown by that merchant. When an observed price reaches your target, Piggy can send a push notification. Piggy never signs into the merchant, places an order, or accesses payment details. Always confirm the final price, availability, delivery fee, and seller terms on the merchant page.

How does cloud backup work?

Sign in from Profile - Cloud backup. Signed-in goals, plans, and activity sync securely to your Piggy account. Guest data stays on the device until you sign in or remove it.

Why did a price check fail?

Some merchants block automated checks or do not publish a machine-readable price. Confirm the link starts with HTTPS and opens directly to one product. Prices and notifications can be delayed.

What is included for free?

The free plan includes up to three active savings goals, one price watch checked daily, planning and progress tools, reminders, widgets, cloud backup, exports, and ads.

What does Piggy Plus add?

Piggy Plus unlocks unlimited savings goals, unlimited price watches, price checks as often as every 30 minutes, and an ad-free experience. Plus pays for convenience and faster information; Piggy still does not hold or invest money.

How do I manage Piggy Plus?

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play. Use Google Play - Payments and subscriptions - Subscriptions to view, cancel, or manage renewal. Use Restore purchases in Piggy if access is missing.

How do I add widgets?

Long-press an empty area of the Android home screen, choose Widgets, find Piggy, then add Goal Glance or Goal Focus. Each widget can follow a different goal.

How do I delete my account?

Use Profile - Cloud backup - Delete account and data, or follow the steps on the account deletion page.
